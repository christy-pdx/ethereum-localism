### Return to Process · Marcus Barrick

**[Representation vs. Reality: Why We Need a Process Economy Now](https://return2process.substack.com/p/representation-vs-reality-why-we)**

Long form reflection and exploration on the intersection of Process Philosophy, Systems Theory, and Buddhism with the meta-crisis. Barrick calls for local and global coordination based on trust, stewardship, and mutual care, rather than commodified transactions.
